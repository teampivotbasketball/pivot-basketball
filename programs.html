Frequently Asked Questions

What equipment does my child need?

Players should bring a basketball, water bottle, and wear athletic shoes and comfortable clothing. We'll provide any additional training equipment needed.

What if my child has never played basketball before?

No problem! Our Youth Development program is perfect for beginners. We meet players at their skill level and focus on fundamentals in a fun, pressure-free environment.

Can my child join mid-season?

Yes, we accept new players throughout the season when space is available. Contact us to discuss enrollment and we'll help your child catch up with the group.
